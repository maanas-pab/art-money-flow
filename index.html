$1,000 Painting
Where does the money go?

You sell an artwork for $1,000. Watch it split into rivers — the gallery takes the widest one. Hover any stream to feel its weight.

Each river's width is proportional to dollars. Data: breakdown.json

The six rivers

Why this split?

The 50% gallery / 15% artist cut is the textbook commercial model — loved by gallerists, mourned by painters. Materials, the actual physical painting, are the thinnest river at 5%. This piece makes that imbalance visceral: you don't read the numbers, you watch the artist's stream run dry.

How to read it

  1. Width = money. Twice as wide, twice as many dollars.
  2. Hover any river to isolate it and read its story.
  3. Drag the price slider — the whole painting re-flows.
